# Current source check — 11 July 2026

Status: narrow refresh, not a complete literature review.

## MIRI illustrative treaty

The public annotated treaty remains explicitly illustrative rather than enacted
or lawyer-vetted. It says its numerical thresholds are best guesses requiring
further study. Its central structure includes:

- preventing ASI and material advancement toward it;
- an ISIA with Conference, Executive Council, and Technical Secretariat;
- training and post-training FLOP limits plus reporting/monitoring bands;
- covered chip clusters, consolidation, chip manufacture/transfer controls,
  research controls, intelligence, challenge inspections, dispute resolution,
  protective action, withdrawal provisions, and rapid technical updates;
- preservation of safely permitted AI benefits under international supervision.

The public text checked on 11 July 2026 defines a covered cluster primarily as
more than sixteen H100-equivalents, co-location, more than 25 Gbit/s aggregate
inter-node bandwidth, or cooperative workload use. The treaty itself notes that
high-bandwidth memory matters but is not the primary compute metric in that
version.

Primary source: <https://ifanyonebuildsit.com/treaty>

## Distributed-training correction

MIRI TGT's April 2026 post proposes an accelerator-memory trigger to make covert
internet-distributed training more operationally exposed. This is best treated
as a later technical correction/variant, not silently attributed to the
currently public annotated treaty text. It affects infrastructure coverage and
detectability rather than proving that distributed training is impossible.

Primary source:
<https://techgov.intelligence.org/blog/catching-illicit-distributed-training-operations-during-an-ai-pause>

## Agreement report

The arXiv report *An International Agreement to Prevent the Premature Creation
of Artificial Superintelligence* remains a primary technical-governance source
for the illustrative agreement and its mechanisms. Exact current version and
differences from the website should be recorded before quoting legal language or
numbers in release material.

Primary source: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.10783>
