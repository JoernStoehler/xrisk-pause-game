# PRC opening authority map

Information date: 11 July 2026.

## Central conclusion

Do not model “China” as one actor. Use two parallel implementation chains:

```text
Party authorization → State Council / civilian ministries / provinces
Party + CMC authorization → military implementation
```

Top leadership can authorize unusually broad action but does not directly
observe site truth. Civilian regulators see different slices; provinces control
much physical implementation; security organs can compel information that may
not be shareable; PLA access requires an explicit parallel order.

## Actor map

- **Central Party leadership/Politburo/PSC/General Secretary:** sets the binding
  political line and tradeoffs; must authorize major foreign inspection,
  military inclusion, destruction/confiscation and strategic concessions.
- **Central Science and Technology Commission / National Security Commission:**
  cross-ministry strategic coordination and adjudication, not routine site
  inspection.
- **State Council/Premier:** turns Party decisions into state regulations,
  ministry assignments, budgets and provincial implementation; not the ordinary
  PLA command chain.
- **CAC:** existing public generative-service, algorithm filing, security and
  data/content machinery. The [2023 Interim
  Measures](http://www.cac.gov.cn/2023-07/13/c_1690898327029107.htm) exclude
  important internal enterprise/research use, so CAC registry is incomplete for
  treaty purposes.
- **MIIT/provincial communications administrations:** industry, electronics,
  telecom, standards, operators, equipment and network visibility; also an
  industrial-development body. Physical workload control needs an explicit
  mandate and system.
- **NDRC/National Data Administration/energy institutions:** projects,
  compute-hub/infrastructure, investment, construction and power cross-checks;
  not workload truth.
- **MPS/local public-security cyber units:** civilian coercive enforcement,
  evidence preservation, seizure/shutdown when law and priority permit.
- **MSS/state-security system:** clandestine, insider, foreign-linked and
  classified leads. Track confidence separately from shareability.
- **CMC/PLA:** separate classified access domain. Civilian compliance is not
  military compliance; CMC/Chair authorization and a military verification
  protocol are explicit state.
- **SASAC/SOEs and provincial SOEs:** shareholder/personnel leverage over
  telecom, grid, cloud, construction and electronics assets, with ownership and
  subsidiary seams.
- **Private labs/cloud firms:** highest-fidelity run, checkpoint, weights and
  research-plan information; Party presence does not imply perfect daily
  command or truthful reporting.
- **Provincial Party/government teams:** land, power, construction, local SOEs
  and enforcement; strongest site knowledge and strong growth/local-champion
  incentives. Use frontier/compute-hub/local-tail classes rather than 31 copies.

## First-six-month implementation graph

1. Top decision explicitly fixes military inclusion, foreign-inspector scope,
   existing inference and seizure/disable authority.
2. Parallel Party/State Council civilian and CMC military orders.
3. Immediate known-site freeze using existing CAC/MIIT relationships and
   resident personnel, before complete inventory.
4. Inventory reconciles CAC filings; MIIT operator/equipment data; NDRC/NDA
   project/hub records; power/construction; firm schedulers/weights/procurement;
   MPS/MSS leads; and separate CMC reporting.
5. Provincial joint teams implement; central randomized inspection and
   cross-dataset reconciliation counter local protection/formalism.
6. ISIA uses negotiated managed access, chain of custody, data minimization,
   challenge procedures and a separate military annex.

## Engine asymmetries

- authority ≠ information;
- compulsion ≠ shareability;
- infrastructure visibility ≠ workload visibility;
- registration ≠ coverage;
- civilian compliance ≠ military compliance;
- Party presence ≠ perfect factual control; and
- foreign inspection is negotiated rather than automatic or impossible.
