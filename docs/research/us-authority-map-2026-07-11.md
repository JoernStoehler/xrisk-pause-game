# US opening authority map

Law snapshot: 11 July 2026. This is a game-research map, not legal advice.

## Central conclusion

Treaty signature and a DG decision do not directly authorize entry into a US
site or coercion of a US lab. The domestic chain is:

```text
President / Congress → valid authority and appropriation → agency order/rule
→ operator implementation → DOJ/court process where coercion is required
```

Voluntary cooperation and control of federal assets can begin sooner. Current
law does not safely supply a freestanding presidential power to seize private
chips/sites or impose a general purely domestic training ban.

## High-value current-law objects

- The [Defense Production Act](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/COMPS-8323/pdf/COMPS-8323.pdf)
  supports priority/allocation programs, capacity support, program-linked
  records/reports/inspection, and supervised voluntary industry agreements.
  The statutory fit of using allocation as a bare negative private-training ban
  is uncertain. The current compilation terminates Titles I, III and most Title
  VII authorities, including §705, on **30 September 2026** unless renewed;
  §708 voluntary agreements are excepted.
- [IEEPA](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?path=/prelim@title50/chapter35&edition=prelim)
  is foreign-interest/transaction oriented; domestic confiscation is much
  narrower than a general compute seizure.
- [Youngstown](https://constitution.congress.gov/browse/essay/artII-S2-C1-1-9/ALDE_00013471/)
  is the core warning against assuming presidential seizure authority without
  statute.
- The [Chemical Weapons Convention Implementation
  Act](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?path=/prelim@title22/chapter75&edition=prelim)
  is a close institutional template: US National Authority, interagency
  coordination, reporting/confidentiality, statutory inspection, warrants,
  penalties and expedited review. It is precedent, not current AI authority.
- Congress controls implementing legislation and appropriations; Senate treaty
  consent alone does not normally create a funded coercive domestic regime.

## Actor map

- **President/White House:** direct federal assets/agencies and procurement;
  negotiate voluntary standstill and international agreement; use valid
  statutory programs. Track executive support separately from statutory hook,
  appropriation and court stay.
- **Congress:** create and fund thresholds, licensing, reporting, telemetry,
  inspection/warrant paths, penalties, emergency interruption, compensated
  acquisition and state/federal structure. It can also block through inaction,
  riders, appointments and DPA nonrenewal.
- **Commerce/BIS:** strong export/foreign-transfer leverage under
  [ECRA/EAR](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?path=/prelim@title50/chapter58&edition=prelim),
  but not a general domestic training regulator. The prior EO 14110 reporting
  architecture was [revoked in January
  2025](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/01/initial-rescissions-of-harmful-executive-orders-and-actions/).
- **DOE/grid institutions:** control DOE/NNSA/lab assets and contracts and add
  power/infrastructure evidence. DOE is not a national private-data-center
  off-switch; utilities, FERC, states and localities remain relevant. Current
  federal policy also accelerates large data-center permitting under [EO
  14318](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/07/accelerating-federal-permitting-of-data-center-infrastructure/).
- **DOD/intelligence/DOJ/FBI:** control federal/contract compute, supply uncertain
  foreign/intelligence leads, investigate and litigate. Intelligence is not a
  domestic industrial-inspection license, and ordinary military policing is
  constrained by [Posse
  Comitatus](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?path=/prelim@title18/part1/chapter67&edition=prelim).
- **Operators:** chip owner, site operator, scheduler controller, model owner,
  colocation landlord and utility may be different. Voluntary/contractual
  control can be immediate. Compelled content/record disclosure interacts with
  the [Stored Communications
  Act](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?path=/prelim@title18/part1/chapter121&edition=prelim).
- **Courts:** warrants, subpoena enforcement, stays, ultra-vires/APA review,
  due process and takings. Court loss can delay or narrow rather than erase a
  regime.
- **States/localities:** heterogeneous utility, permitting, emergency,
  procurement, police and property authority; federal preemption and
  anti-commandeering matter.

## First-six-month sequence

- Days 0–7: federal-run halt, voluntary lab/cloud standstill and observers,
  export/foreign-access freeze, triage of credible crime/foreign-linked cases.
- Weeks 1–4: possible DPA §708 voluntary agreement; implementing/renewal bill;
  voluntary monitors can arrive before compulsory authority.
- Months 1–3: treaty implementation and DPA renewal before 30 September;
  appointments, appropriations, rules and first litigation.
- Months 3–6: new licensing/reporting/warrant-backed inspections can scale if
  Congress acted. Acquisition/consolidation still requires authority, money,
  compensation and logistics.

## Required action schema

Every US action should specify:

`decision maker; legal basis; implementer; controlled asset; voluntary or
compulsory; coverage; lead time; evidence needed; confidentiality/privacy cost;
appropriation; challenge/stay risk; sunset; reversibility`.

Unresolved legal branches include DPA allocation fit, EAR treatment of weights
and algorithms, telemetry status under the SCA, administrative-inspection
limits, state emergency/utility variation, acquisition compensation, DPA
renewal, and whether ISIA operates through a US National Authority.
