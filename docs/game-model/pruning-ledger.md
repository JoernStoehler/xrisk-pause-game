# Pruning ledger

Status: **design choices, not claims that omitted mechanisms are unimportant in
reality**.

The release engine should spend complexity where it changes the policy argument,
creates an intelligible decision, or prevents a materially false lesson. Text
and code volume are cheap; player attention and expert-review bandwidth are not.

## Retain explicitly

| Mechanism | Why it earns model complexity |
|---|---|
| Persistent correlated hidden worlds | Converts model uncertainty into replayable variation without arbitrary punishment. |
| Rights/control/use/verification separation | Central to explaining what “ban” and “monitoring” operationally mean. |
| Compute-or-memory and substitution surfaces | Prevents a compute threshold from masquerading as complete control. |
| Latent conduct versus evidence | Needed for treaty verification, false alarms, concealment, and reciprocal confidence. |
| Multi-actor implementation | Prevents “the US” or “China” from acting as unitary reliable agents. |
| Distinct crisis pathways | War, bargaining, agenda displacement, and physical-access loss have different warnings and remedies. |
| Route- and carrier-specific reinvestment | Prevents a scalar capability multiplier from manufacturing RSI. |
| Candidate/threat/evidence/evaluator safety review | Makes pause exit a decision under error rather than a safety-progress threshold. |
| Continuing post-treaty-failure process | Teaches how loss of restraint creates an unsafe build instead of declaring failure synonymous with extinction. |

## Compress, but preserve interfaces

| Territory | Game representation | Lost detail / guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Macroeconomy | Sectoral benefits, costs, inference demand, fiscal/coalition effects | Not a general equilibrium model. Do not infer precise GDP effects. |
| Public opinion | Constituencies, salient incidents, elite cues, mobilization, coarse support | Not individual voters or survey-level forecasts. Effects must route through actors and institutions. |
| Military geopolitics | Named crises, authority/access/evidence changes, escalation stages | Not an operational war game. Preserve causal differences among war paths. |
| Intelligence | Search tasks, sources, sensor families, rival hypotheses, response latency | No universal “CIA skill” number. Keep false positives, blind spots, and source dependence. |
| Capability | General factor-like persistent frontier plus ragged profiles and projects | No monthly benchmark forecast; no benchmark directly equals RSI readiness. |
| Research | Typed projects/task graphs, resources, artifacts, evaluation, carrier scope | No simulation of scientific content. Avoid generic research points becoming truth. |
| International system | US, China, ISIA, strategically relevant third parties | Not every state. Third parties enter when they host resources, supply chains, sanctions, or crisis leverage. |
| Law | Authority, vetoes, exceptions, timing, implementation organizations | Not a complete constitutional/legal model. |
| Organizations | Task-specific skill, authority, information edges, incentives, continuity | Not every officeholder. Avoid one scalar competence or capture variable. |

## Defer unless testing shows high discourse value

- full supply-chain industrial simulation below the level needed for chip,
  memory, networking, power, and reconstruction choices;
- detailed electoral geography and party competition;
- tactical Taiwan-war outcomes beyond effects on treaty operation;
- precise corporate finance and model-service pricing;
- endogenous scientific disciplines and publication networks;
- procedural law below decision-relevant veto, delay, seizure, and review paths;
- a player-managed ISIA org chart with individual hiring for every role;
- fine-grained diplomacy with states that have no relevant compute, supply-chain,
  enforcement, or alliance role in the sampled run.

## Omit from the playable causal engine

- exact simulated biochemistry, nanotechnology, or weapon design;
- a present-technology-only extinction mode;
- detailed benchmark leaderboards as capability truth;
- a probability-of-survival meter;
- outcomes where unsafe superintelligence permanently coexists with independent
  humanity;
- a treaty-breakdown-as-instant-game-over rule;
- arbitrary independent disaster cards added merely for difficulty;
- an exhaustive ontology of every possible alignment proposal.

## Unresolved architecture edges

These need expert judgment before strong numerical weight or irreversible UX
commitments:

1. whether restricted hosted inference is a stable policy regime, and which
   dangerous existing-model uses dominate on each time scale;
2. when an authorized inference episode itself subsumes the external
   improvement-versus-response race;
3. which technical winning routes are objectively possible and recognizably
   adequate before the decisive build;
4. whether deliberate pause weakening under a fully internalized near-term
   extinction belief is a distinct behavioral family or a label for decomposable
   failures;
5. the opening-year relative incidence of event families under different
   control packages;
6. which mechanisms deserve direct cards and choices versus background state
   transitions.

## Calibration ledger template

Numerical values should be added only with a row like this:

| Parameter/event family | Conditional on | Evidence/argument | Elicited from | Confidence in sign | Confidence in magnitude | Game sensitivity | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| example: monitor installation delay | site access, staffing, authority, jurisdiction | source or structured judgment | expert/model | … | … | … | uncalibrated |

Absence of a number is preferable to a number whose scope and conditioning are
unclear. Calibration should begin with distributions over observable opening-
year events and paired policy effects, not with one annual “treaty survival” or
“alignment success” probability.
