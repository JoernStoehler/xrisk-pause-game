## Current route judgment

It is difficult to forecast which route to safe superintelligence works. Jörn's
present view is not that designed cognition, uploads/enhancement and
control-assisted learned systems are three similarly live candidates. It is:

- none of the concrete routes currently brainstormed looks adequate;
- a successful route would require institution-building beyond historical
  precedent;
- Jörn's leading bet is that it involves human uploading and a project operated
  by intelligence-augmented humans whose cognition avoids important classes of
  ordinary human error.

“Human-derived” does not itself establish safety. The conjectured advantage is
that enhanced researchers may be able to construct and audit a solution without
the reasoning failures that defeat present institutions.

## Institutional adequacy

Chernobyl and nuclear non-proliferation are offered as comparatively easy
coordination and reliability problems that nevertheless failed in multiple
ways. A superalignment project must succeed under harder epistemic,
organizational, security, dual-use and one-shot pressures.

An important selection criterion is the ability to reject wishful reasoning and
to predict failure. Paraphrasing Yudkowsky, the needed team contains people who
say a proposal will not work and are then correct when it does not work. Mere
prestige—company, university or prize-winning status—is not adequate evidence
of this competence. Jörn judges the existing track record of such institutions
and experts on ASI alignment to be negative and therefore not evidence that they
will succeed under greater pressure on a first irreversible attempt.
