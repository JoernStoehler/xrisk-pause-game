## Separate danger from breach

A prohibited action, a capability gain, loss of effective human control, and harm are different stages. A leaked insight can change future feasibility without creating a model; a detected run may be halted; a withdrawing party may return before a dangerous build. Conversely, an **authorized** system may be unsafe, and an improvement inside permitted inference might leave little warning time. This is the archived atlas's causal distinction, not a measured frequency: `git show 2552820:docs/expert-model/loss-of-control-and-manifestation.md` (“Treaty failure is not yet game over,” “Paths into a dangerous build”) and `docs/expert-model/capabilities-and-reinvestment.md` (“Live pathways to improvement”).

## Authority and evidence are actor-specific

“The state” is not one player. The July 2026 US map found voluntary/federal-site steps could precede compulsory private-site inspection, which needs a domestic legal, funding, and court path. The PRC map separated civilian Party/State Council/provincial implementation from CMC/PLA access, and distinguished infrastructure visibility from workload truth. Both are **dated, jurisdiction-specific research maps** (`git show 2552820:docs/research/us-authority-map-2026-07-11.md` and `...:docs/research/prc-authority-map-2026-07-11.md`); recheck law and practice before using them. They illustrate general questions, not a fixed US–China founding scenario. Other states, companies, chip supply actors, international staff, and people holding private evidence may matter under different threat assumptions.

Evidence moves through **conduct → trace → collection → interpretation → lawful response**. A power anomaly may be innocent; a clean inspection may miss moved equipment; classified intelligence may be strong but unshareable. For any response, ask who controls the asset, who can authorize entry or shutdown, how quickly they can act, whether the signal can be corroborated, and what the intervention itself costs. [Verification methods, “Limitations and Considerations”](../literature/wasil-verification-methods-ai-agreements-2024.md) supports the detection/access limits; the chain is archived project synthesis in `docs/game-model/causal-atlas.md`, “Compute-control chain.”

The strongest live uncertainty is which route is both feasible and faster than institutional response. Distributed and smaller-model routes may bind in some worlds; concentrated frontier clusters may remain decisive in others. Similarly, broad inference may strengthen resilience and safety work or erode the perimeter. The retained sources do not settle prevalence, covert detection, halt latency, political willingness, or safe-exit adequacy. Discriminating work would test specific hardware/topology assumptions, audit actual access and stopping powers, compare bounded inference policies, and ask Jörn to judge the conditioned threat model. Avoid turning diagnostic fixtures, historical analogies, or a source's proposed scenario into a forecast.
