# Selective expert-to-game crosswalk

Status: only high-leverage interfaces. This is not an attempt to translate every
expert-model claim into a game variable.

| Expert-model claim or uncertainty | Evidence / judgment | Game approximation | Compression / distortion | Derivation direction | Failure test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Within-paradigm capability is correlated and ragged; paradigm changes can replace the map | Primary scaling/benchmark evidence plus Jörn/project synthesis; exact citations in source index | Persistent capability-production hypothesis, general factor-like profile, domain/task thresholds, paradigm events | Month-level benchmark forecasting and full task ontology discarded | Expert → game | A benchmark score must not directly set RSI readiness or lethal threshold |
| RSI may proceed through internal cognition, explicit research, trained successors, labs, hardware or economic routes; it may fizzle | Yudkowsky structural model, research-production evidence, Jörn frontier judgment | Candidate project/task graphs, carrier transitions, resources, evaluation, implementation and response time | Actual scientific content and a fitted open-ended tail law discarded | Expert → game; route enumeration partly reconstructed | Same starting capability must permit bounded, delayed, distributed and fast routes for named reasons |
| Existing inference jointly affects useful services, political opposition, research, offense and evasion | Jörn judgment plus cross-domain project conjecture; task-level magnitude unresolved | Cold/restricted/broad policy plus workload limits and several mediator processes | Precise GDP/service demand and task-level market equilibrium discarded | Expert → game, pending review of signs/timing | Political benefit cannot directly cancel technical risk as one utility number |
| Compute prohibition works through ownership, custody, credentials, power, monitoring, authority and response | MIRI treaty mechanisms, safeguards practice, project synthesis | Typed asset/control records and action-to-implementation pipeline | Full electrical/semiconductor engineering below decision relevance discarded | Source/expert → game | Legal prohibition cannot set actual workload to zero by fiat |
| Verification separates conduct, trace, collection, interpretation, reporting and response | Safeguards practice, organizational literature, historical process evidence | Latent conduct, evidence objects, source dependencies, recipient-specific beliefs and authority | Exhaustive sensor physics and intelligence operations discarded | Source → expert synthesis → game | Support violation without alert, alert without violation, and true report not reaching DG |
| Treaty persistence has several mechanisms and failure paths, not one health variable | International-relations/organizational mechanisms plus Jörn examples | Asymmetric actors, authority/information graph, exemptions, constituencies, crisis subtypes and continuity | Officeholder-level politics and general political microsimulation discarded | Source/expert → game | Leader belief loss, subordinate action and physical-access loss must be distinct traces |
| Safety is candidate/threat specific; evidence, evaluator judgment, institutional approval and authorization can diverge | Alignment objections, assurance ontology, Jörn/project review; positive winning routes unresolved | Candidate/claim/evidence/evaluator/review/authorization/build process | Internal content of a true alignment solution discarded | Expert negative constraints → game; positive branches partly fixture | Support safe-but-rejected, unsafe-but-approved, approved-but-unauthorized and authorized-but-unimplemented cases |
| Unsafe decisive capability is not bounded to current attack technology, but still requires access and manifestation | Yudkowsky structural model plus Jörn's explicit realism requirement; exact access/timing unresolved | Capability/access acquisition → control defeat → abstract physical manifestation → extinction | Exact novel science, weapon design and time-to-extinction discarded | Expert → game terminal process; manifestations partly authored | Treaty failure cannot end the run immediately; extinction must require a traceable dangerous-build, access and manifestation path without implying present technology is the ceiling |
| The real expert model lacks defensible probabilities for many ASI-specific paths | Evidence limitation and explicit project methodology | Persistent hypothesis families, broad distributions only after elicitation, unweighted stress scenarios | Pretense of one authoritative calibrated joint prior rejected | Expert limitation constrains game calibration | Every runtime number records forecast, expert judgment, fixture or playability provenance |

## When to expand the crosswalk

Add a row only when an expert disagreement, new policy option, player confusion,
or model sensitivity shows that a simplification can change a policy ranking,
warning sign, reachable terminal path, or central public lesson.
