## Review-state vocabulary

- **source-checked:** an exact claim is tied to a present source;
- **mechanism-reviewed:** reviewer accepted or repaired the causal distinction,
  not its probability;
- **forecast-reviewed:** conditioning and an ordinal/numeric assessment were
  reviewed;
- **game-reviewed:** compression and behavior were checked against the atlas;
- **fixture-only:** needed for execution/play but unsupported as a forecast;
- **missing:** no adequate present treatment; and
- **deliberately pruned:** a recorded scope decision with a deletion test.

Do not shorten “missing because corpus absent” to “unknown”; the corrective
action is restoration, not frontier elicitation.
