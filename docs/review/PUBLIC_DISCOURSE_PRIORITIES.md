# Public-discourse teaching priorities

Status: product-research synthesis, 11 July 2026. Ordinal judgments are for
review, not expert-approved claims, forecasts, or a claim about public-opinion
prevalence. Evidence and method are in
`docs/research/public-discourse-audit-2026-07-11.md`.

## Recommendation

Make the game's distinctive teaching object **the operational work of a pause**,
not another generic argument that advanced AI could be dangerous.

The audit team's leading candidate autonomous artifact is a compact set of paired, playable or traceable
cases in which the treaty text and hidden event are held constant while
observation, evidence routing, authority, access, interpretation, response or
legitimacy differ. This targets a cross-stratum omission selected by auditor judgment and
uses gameplay's comparative advantage: players can act under partial
information, experience delays and trade-offs, and inspect a retrospective.

Keep the strongest opposition inside those cases. A regime can reduce risk and
still be captured, discriminatory, privacy-invasive, strategically unstable or
too slow. The desired lesson is not “the institution works”; it is “feasibility
and failure are mechanism questions that can be reasoned about.”

This ranking is an auditor judgment, not a measured discourse-frequency or
expected-impact estimate.

## Ordinal rubric

Each target is judged on six dimensions. Labels deliberately avoid numerical
scores and are not summed.

- **Sample salience:** high / medium / low structured reviewer judgment about
  recurrence in this purposive sample.
- **Conditional consequence:** catastrophic / major / moderate effect on
  reasoning or policy choice if the relevant threat model is true.
- **Claim confidence:** strong external support / bounded support / project or
  expert draft / unresolved.
- **Potential decision relevance:** high / medium / low judgment about whether a
  plausible contrast can reverse a meaningful institutional choice; no
  likelihood was measured.
- **Gameplay advantage:** exceptional / good / limited advantage over prose.
- **Wrong-lesson risk:** high / medium / low if simplified badly.

Priority tiers are ordinal portfolio judgments:

- **P1 — center the first comparative artifact**
- **P2 — integrate early and test**
- **P3 — represent selectively or after a threat-model gate**
- **Hold — do not teach as settled**

Within-tier letters do not express an ordering.

## Recommended prototype/content changes

1. Reframe the current opening slice as one candidate **institutional reasoning
   laboratory**, not “the first real gameplay loop.” Its review value is paired
   causal traces, not polish or campaign breadth.
2. Author three paired cases before expanding progression:
   - same covert activity, different evidence reach and legal actionability;
   - same suspicious signal, different truth and clarification/escalation path;
   - same compute prohibition, different custody, substitution and political
     legitimacy.
   Add one comparison holding the institution constant while varying the threat
   model/adversary, and one where control endogenously changes evasion or
   substitution. At least one diagnostic stress world should make catastrophic
   capability slower or less likely and let opportunity cost, concentration,
   conflict, or safer competitive development dominate; it is not a forecast.
3. Add an end-of-case retrospective that separately displays designer-known
   conduct, traces, recipients, beliefs, decisions, consequences and remaining
   uncertainty. Label any omniscient diagnostic view as unavailable in-world.
4. Distribute capture, surveillance, benefits, sovereignty and overhang
   objections among multiple competent actors whose warnings are sometimes
   outcome-relevant and correct. Include cases where the default pro-pause or
   institutional recommendation is wrong.
5. De-emphasize generic extinction persuasion, benchmark/scaling exposition,
   and treaty-signing drama until the mechanism-learning cases are tested.
6. Test comprehension and transfer with humans before concluding that sampled
   discourse gaps are player misconceptions or that the game corrects them.

## Autonomous work and review surface

Autonomous work can safely produce: a frozen/coded corpus; paired trace scripts;
claim-status and source ledgers; misconception/transfer questions; adversarial
alternative interpretations; UI prototypes for evidence and retrospectives;
and a playtest protocol. It cannot establish expert truth without Jörn, player
learning without participants, or market demand without audience evidence.

The most efficient Jörn review surface is not this audit. Send one mechanism
crux at a time with two short traces, the strongest rival interpretation, and
the exact decision his answer changes. Reserve broad source tables for audit.

## Questions ordinary research cannot settle

Do not ask these until autonomous source/model work has produced a concrete
paired trace and shown that the answer changes an implementation choice.

1. Under your current model, which **minimum control omission** most plausibly
   turns a serious signed pause into false reassurance: inference/weights,
   custody/stop authority, undeclared resources, research scope, or crisis
   continuity?
2. Which paired contrast would most distort your model if we teach it first:
   signal versus truth, authority versus factual control, or imperfect
   verification versus no useful control?
3. For a safe-exit case, should the first artifact teach only that objective
   adequacy, evidence, recognition, authorization and implementation differ, or
   is there one candidate route concrete enough to portray without implying your
   approval?
