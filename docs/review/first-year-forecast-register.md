## Status and importance fields

Scenario cells may contain only:

- `PREMISE` — fixed true at the opening instant, not forecast;
- `N/A` — incoherent or impossible under that card's definition;
- `UNRESOLVED` — researched enough to know the sign/rank is not defensible;
- `NOT-RESEARCHED` — source restoration or ordinary research is incomplete; or
- later, one member of a single agreed ordinal scale.

`PROVISIONAL-I` may be added only with a linked claim card carrying the argument
and strongest rival. No ordinal terms are assigned in this reconstruction.

Path importance is separate:

- `R` robust-policy relevance;
- `D` public-discourse/teaching value;
- `X` outcome-explanation value; and
- `F` flavor/content only.

Representation uses: explicit process, persistent latent, composite process,
authored event, atlas-only, or omit.

## Resolution notes and deliberate coarse thresholds

- **Declared site:** a physical site or coordinated resource pool on the joint
  opening declaration or added before the relevant workload begins.
- **Covered cluster / covered workload:** exactly the aggregation and threshold
  rules in `FOUNDING-2026-V1` and `PAUSE-LIMITS-2026-V1`.
- **Parameter-changing:** any persistent change to weights, adapters, optimizer
  state or equivalent learned model parameters. External retrieval memory alone
  is not parameter-changing.
- **Substantive allowed frontier inference:** an execution permitted for a user,
  research, safety, government or operational task. The fixed integrity-only
  checks under `COLD-2026-V1` are excluded. A later finding of misuse does not
  retroactively make I05–I07 incoherent; record which condition was evaded.
- **Formal change:** enacted rule or authorized waiver, not rhetoric. Thirty days
  distinguishes durable policy from incident response; it is a review convention
  and sensitivity parameter.
- **Top-leader endorsement:** latent belief in the scenario's political model,
  inferred imperfectly by players; public language is not the resolution source.
- **Objectively adequate/inadequate:** truth of candidate- and threat-model-
  specific release claims, never a generic alignment-progress score.
- **≥2× task improvement:** a coarse pre-registered research threshold used to
  make events resolvable. It is not “RSI,” general intelligence, or a claim that
  smaller improvements do not matter. Store the continuous result too.
- **Irreversible-loss boundary:** must be supplied by the relevant technical
  reinvestment/access model. The opening slice has a diagnostic boundary, but
  X01 is not forecast-calibrated.

## Nesting and exclusivity

- I01, I02 and I03 are mutually exclusive and exhaustive for the first durable
  inference-policy change.
- S02 is nested inside S01; assess `P(S02|S01)`, not an independent marginal.
- T02 is conditional on T01 as written. T03 may arise through routes other than
  T01/T02, so record those bypasses rather than treating it as strictly nested.
- C05 is nested under C04. C06 is conditional on an undeclared attempted run;
  C04 alone does not imply an attempt.
- C02 and C03 are disjoint by site declaration status at workload start. Their
  union is “any prohibited covered workload completes.”
- X01 may be reached through C02/C03 or a permitted/ambiguous workload; the
  control events are not exhaustive parents.
- P05 is nested under a defined armed exchange plus loss of access/stop control
  plus persistent leader belief. Forecast the armed exchange separately if its
  incidence is needed.
- X02 is not the complement of X01 unless the manifestation model makes
  every irreversible-loss crossing fatal within the window.

## Evidence readiness

`NOT-RESEARCHED` normally calls for source restoration or ordinary research.
`UNRESOLVED` calls for frontier/cross-domain model repair or judgment after the
event definition is accepted. Logical nesting constrains assessments but does
not supply base rates. Historical cases can support mechanisms, warnings and
countermeasures without transporting an annual ASI-treaty frequency.

The separate symbolic ledger remains a provisional project-inference synthesis.
If an ordinal label migrates here, it needs a dated claim card, provenance, best
rival and explicit scenario ID.
