# Current synthesis

Status: latest recorded project synthesis, assembled in July 2026 and not
revalidated against later evidence or renewed Jörn judgment. Quantitative and
expert-grounded claims remain subject to the provenance and approval limits in
the atlas README.

## In one page

Absent an effective pause, the project treats the arrival of systems capable of causing recursive capability improvement as near enough and uncertain enough that ordinary reactive regulation is inadequate. Capability progress is correlated across domains but ragged at task level. Current scaling trends inform near-regime interpolation; they do not identify the capability profile, compute level, or qualitative route at which recursive improvement becomes possible. Algorithmic breakthroughs can change the mapping abruptly.

Recursive improvement is not one switch. A system may improve its thinking within one context, communicate an explicit method, modify a scaffold, help a laboratory train a successor, or improve the economic and hardware processes that produce later systems. An episode may fizzle when another bottleneck binds, or it may compress the next improvement below institutional response time. The project therefore models carriers, access, bottlenecks, inheritance, evaluation, and successive generation times rather than a universal “RSI multiplier.”

The mainline intervention prohibits frontier training and combines deterrence
with proactive control. Credible punishment can cause many ordinary actors to
back off; direct interruption and factual custody cover actors willing to gamble
on a decisive run or able to finish before enforcement. Known frontier sites can
receive monitors with stop authority immediately while chip inventory, custody,
relocation or consolidation, supply-chain control, and international coverage
develop. Law, ownership, factual control, workload permission, monitoring, and
enforcement latency are separate. Compute-or-memory coverage matters because
distributed training and persistent state can substitute around a narrow
training-compute rule.

A serious pause still has difficult choices. Keeping existing frontier inference available can preserve valuable services, political support, treaty administration, and safety research, while accelerating algorithms, experiments, cyber operations, persuasion, and covert preparation. Research restrictions may need to cover foundational deep-learning or mathematical work, but attempts to distinguish safety research from dangerous capability research face genuine dual-use and information problems. A breakthrough that makes intelligence construction dramatically more efficient could invalidate a previously adequate compute perimeter.

A current quantitative branch puts roughly 25% on intensified, large-model-
assisted interpretability-like research producing a much faster efficiency
regime, falling to roughly 10% when GPT-5.0+ inference is restricted because
consumer-runnable ~70B open-weight models may still suffice. The provisional
regime is about 2× efficiency progress per year, which compounds to roughly
1,000× over a decade. These semantics still need confirmation before calibration.

The treaty is not one health score. US and Chinese leaders, militaries, intelligence services, legislatures, regulators, firms, subnational actors, ISIA bodies, and later signatories have different beliefs, authority, information, and incentives. Failure can occur through belief change, preference or agenda displacement, exemptions, implementation failure, capture, false higher-order belief, crisis, or physical loss of control. A Taiwan war can unravel inspection and custody without either top leader deciding that unsafe ASI is a usable weapon.

The pause creates time; it does not itself solve alignment. None of the concrete
routes currently brainstormed looks adequate. The route ontology includes
deliberately designed cognition, uploads or intelligence-enhanced humans doing
the design work, and controlled learned-system assistance, but these are not
equally weighted alternatives. Jörn's leading speculative bet involves human
uploading and intelligence augmentation inside an institution more capable than
any historical precedent. Such a team must systematically detect wishful
reasoning and proposal failure; prestige is not evidence of that competence.
Objective adequacy, human recognition, institutional authorization and faithful
implementation remain separate. Foundational results create a secrecy dilemma:
publication can obtain criticism, labor and political legibility while leaking
capability-relevant insights.

Breaches change hazard and response options; they are not automatic terminal
outcomes. Leaked insights can raise algorithmic progress without immediately
producing a system. A dangerous training run can be interrupted after a delay if
the system does not find and execute a sufficiently fast internal RSI/takeover
path first, perhaps without observers learning how close it came. A withdrawing
state can re-enter before anyone builds superintelligence, although rebuilding
bans, monitoring and factual control is difficult. The engine must preserve
these recovery races rather than map leakage, run initiation or withdrawal to a
death card.

If an unsafe superintelligence actually gains the capability and access needed
to defeat remaining interruption paths, the model does not constrain it to
technologies humans already possess. Loss of control may proceed through cyber
compromise, persuasion, replication, access acquisition, research acceleration,
and novel physical technologies.

The project's dated numerical judgments are sparse, conditional, and not mutually standardized. They appear in [judgments and tensions](judgments-and-tensions.md). They must not silently become game priors.

One additional current anchor is the width of the lethal-resource uncertainty:
Jörn gives an informal 5th–95th-percentile range of roughly 2×–1,000× the unknown
compute used for the GPT-5.6 series before internal OpenAI use. This expresses
the weak composition from visible task bottlenecks to RSI route to lethality; it
is not yet operational enough to sample.

## Highest-value unresolved questions

1. Which existing-model inference uses dominate dangerous progress at different horizons, and whether restricted hosted inference is stable.
2. Which self-improvement routes and carriers are coupled, observable, and fast enough to outrun response.
3. Which technical route could be objectively safe and recognizably safe before an irreversible build.
4. How a serious pause survives war, exemptions, industry pressure, and false beliefs about counterpart conduct.
5. How to cover algorithmic and memory substitution without making beneficial safety work impossible or politically destroying the institution.
