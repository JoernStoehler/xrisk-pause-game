## Decision use

This ledger selects the next bounded result most likely to change project resource allocation. Update it when contrastive evidence changes the ranking; do not let an available implementation or the most recent artifact silently become the priority.

This allocation is an intermediate development choice, not the project
objective or a stopping condition. Identifying or preparing an experiment does

## Current allocation

Prepare a low-cost, human-testable interaction experiment for one operational-control crux before another production UI or campaign build. The experiment should compare materially different compact interaction grammars while holding the lesson, underlying case, prose budget, and evidence boundary as constant as practicable.

The decision it must inform is narrower than product shape:

> Is there a mobile-scale interaction in which premise-qualified newcomers feel consequential agency and can later explain why authority, implementation, evidence, and factual control diverged?

An agent-built artifact can make this testable and reject obvious failures. Only fresh target-human use can supply evidence about felt agency, comfortable reading, cold comprehension, or transfer.

## Why this ranking

- The [success model](SUCCESS_MODEL.md) identifies felt agency and transfer as necessary adjacent links in the project impact route and records no human evidence for either.
- The [game-design portfolio](../game-design/README.md) proposes an institutional-competence thriller and several complementary loop families, but labels their ratings as design judgment rather than measured evidence.
- The [public-discourse synthesis](../review/PUBLIC_DISCOURSE_PRIORITIES.md) favors paired operational cases because gameplay can expose divergence among rule, evidence, authority, response, and control; the underlying audit is purposive, not a prevalence estimate.
- The [product architecture portfolio](PRODUCT_ARCHITECTURE_PORTFOLIO.md) keeps integrated, layered, and separate use contexts live rather than selecting one.
- The [review routing](../review/REVIEW_ROUTING.md) separates game feel, transfer, readability, visual craft, product shape, and engineering; a verdict in one lane cannot approve the others.
- The [fresh-root handoff](../../FRESH_ROOT_HANDOFF.md) and [postmortem](../../SESSION_POSTMORTEM_2026-07-11.md) show that implementation and narrow review previously outran an explicit product priority and complete quality frame.

## Experiment constraints

The first artifact should:

- use one named diagnostic case, not a forecast or representative frequency;
- compare at least two genuinely different interaction grammars, not cosmetic variants;
- keep visible prose comfortably readable at real-phone scale and progressive;
- preserve requested action, authority, implementation receipt, evidence, Director belief, and retrospective truth as distinct where relevant;
- give the player a consequential action and visible world change without an aggregate competence or survival score;
- include a fair causal retrospective unavailable to the Director during play;
- test one transfer target and one likely false lesson;
- avoid production art investment beyond what is needed for credible comprehension and visual preflight; and
- produce a participant-ready protocol that does not coach the intended answer.

The leading comparison is not yet fixed. Plausible variants include cabinet case drama plus a small operational manipulation, versus a compact evidence/casefile sequence. A third static paired-trace laboratory may be useful as a cheaper learning comparator rather than another game candidate.

## Strongest live alternatives

1. **Resolve the expert-model crux first.** This wins if no bounded case can be drafted without a domain choice that materially changes the interaction or lesson.
2. **Establish visual direction first.** This wins if low-fidelity presentation would so strongly confound willingness to engage that loop evidence would be uninterpretable, or if a reusable art-direction comparison can be tested more cheaply than interaction variants.
3. **Test facilitated case use first.** This wins if recruitment or prior research shows voluntary mobile entry is currently inaccessible while a real educator can immediately run a case, making facilitation the cheaper route to learning evidence.
4. **Use a prose or static explainer comparator before game variants.** This wins if the primary uncertainty is whether the operational distinction can be taught at all, rather than whether interaction adds agency or transfer.

## Reversal and escalation conditions

Change the allocation when any of the following occurs:

- a concrete case exposes a Jörn-only expert judgment that changes the action set, causal lesson, or fairness of the reveal;
- structural preflight cannot fit a meaningful decision and receipt into comfortable phone-scale reading without teaching a shortcut;
- target-human evidence shows neither interaction variant produces agency or causal explanation, in which case test a cheaper static/facilitated form before more game production;
- a low-cost visual comparison shows presentation dominates engagement enough that interaction results would be confounded;
- a facilitator commits to a real near-term use context that supplies better evidence than an unguided prototype; or
- new evidence contradicts the operational-middle teaching priority or identifies a more consequential premise-qualified misconception.
