# Product architecture portfolio

Status: product decision framework, 11 July 2026. It does not approve
expert-grounded content, select a final product shape, or establish player
learning or market demand. Its product recommendation has not been revalidated
since July.

## Decision now

Preserve **one shared causal substrate and one public identity**, but keep the
packaging decision open. The next evidence-producing artifact should be one
short, mobile-first Director episode with concrete implementation receipts,
delayed callbacks and an optional, clearly separate same-world retrospective.
It should be able to become the first campaign episode, a standalone paired
case, or the shared core of layered modes without a rewrite.

This is a reversible default, not a selection of layered modes. Do not yet
build a broad campaign, a second public product, or a permanent mode system.
First test whether the operational chain is legible, interesting and
transferable:

```text
decision -> authority -> implementation -> factual control
         -> evidence -> interpretation -> response -> delayed consequence
```

Keep the expert atlas, symbolic model and review/pruning interface as distinct
production artifacts. They may support the public product, but their existence
does not imply that players should see their complexity.

## Fixed product contract

- **Fixed premise:** humanity's survival is the terminal objective.
  Superintelligence is possible and is lethal if it is not developed safely;
  target players already grant both claims. Generic extinction persuasion is
  outside the primary loop. Unsafe superintelligence and extinction must remain
  a causal terminal process, not a generic score or instant consequence of
  treaty trouble.
- **Expert judgment:** Jörn's current view is that without an adequate pause we
  are almost certainly dead. This is an expert-grounded directional premise for
  the product, not a diagnostic frequency, calibrated playable probability or
  generic UI meter.
- **Fixed premise:** the United States and China begin a ban treaty because
  their top leadership accepts the lethal risk; ISIA exists; and the player is
  its Director. This conditions the scenario and is not a forecast that treaty
  formation is easy or imminent.
- **Fixed premise:** the Director wants the treaty to succeed. The product does
  not perform fake neutrality about whether extinction or treaty success is
  desirable.
- **Fixed premise:** this does not make every pro-treaty action correct.
  Competent actors may correctly identify capture, rights, sovereignty,
  escalation, foregone-benefit, evasion, substitution or safe-exit failures.
  The US and China are asymmetric, non-unitary institutions rather than
  mirrored national personalities.
- **Fixed premise:** UI simplicity is an architectural constraint. One primary
  decision, a small known-state surface and progressive disclosure take
  priority over an all-purpose dashboard. Epistemically distinct views must
  remain visibly distinct.

The live uncertainty is about what an adequate pause must prohibit, control,
observe and survive; how it can be implemented and repaired; and what evidence
could justify a safe exit. It is not a neutral race-versus-pause balance. The
game can test whether particular controls are sufficient, harmful or
misdesigned without treating continued unsafe development as an equally valid
terminal objective.

The stable public lesson is not “the institution works.” It is that feasibility
and failure are mechanism questions: law, authority, implementation, factual
control, evidence, interpretation and response can diverge while a pause
remains active crisis management.

length, a successful deployment and branch frequencies are not north stars.

## Shape portfolio

The engine/content boundary should not decide the packaging. All three shapes
can share typed actions, receipts, causal traces, claim records and the rule
that hidden truth never leaks into the Director's view.

### One integrated game

A continuous Director campaign contains operational choices, delayed
consequences, explanation, progression and eventual terminal paths.

- **Impact advantage:** identification and accumulated consequences may turn
  individual mechanisms into the felt work of sustaining an institution.
- **Architectural risk:** campaign progression, caveats, causal explanation
  and political/scientific breadth may overload a phone or make uncertainty
  feel like arbitrary punishment.
- **Evidence that favors it:** several linked cases remain legible; players
  voluntarily continue; earlier decisions create understandable later options;
  and transfer is at least as strong inside the campaign as in isolated cases.
- **Evidence that freezes it:** those results repeat across target-player
  cohorts and real devices, while content production sustains claim review and
  causal callbacks without generic meters.
- **Evidence to pivot away:** players need an out-of-role explanation to
  understand most consequences, cannot resume the causal thread, or campaign
  needs repeatedly force cards to mutate hidden truth directly.

### Separate public products

A short Director game, paired-case reasoning laboratory and/or public explainer
are independently usable products, even if they reuse a model and content
pipeline.

- **Impact advantage:** each audience can receive the depth and pacing suited
  to its task; paired cases can optimize precise mechanism transfer without
  carrying a campaign.
- **Architectural risk:** duplicated content and claim review, fragmented public
  identity, cross-product navigation and asking newcomers to select a format
  before they understand the premise.
- **Evidence that favors it:** paired cases produce transfer but little desire
  for continuing play; campaign players systematically avoid necessary review;
  or audience research finds distinct stable use contexts that one shell serves
  poorly.
- **Evidence that freezes it:** two thin products each demonstrate independent
  demand and learning value, share event/claim records without forks, and have
  explicit ownership and maintenance capacity.
- **Evidence to pivot away:** the products duplicate authoring, drift in causal
  claims, split a small audience, or require each other to make sense.

### Layered modes in one public product

A simple Director campaign is the default; dossier, retrospective,
same-world comparison and perhaps a compact case laboratory are progressively
disclosed modes in the same shell.

- **Impact advantage:** low-friction role-play and auditable depth can share one
  identity and causal record without appearing simultaneously.
- **Architectural risk:** optional explanation may become required explanation;
  mode switches may blur Director knowledge with designer-known truth; one app
  may conceal the maintenance cost of several products.
- **Evidence that favors it:** optional retrospective materially improves
  explanation or transfer without reducing core completion; players understand
  why the epistemic boundary changes; and interested players seek depth without
  confusing it with the main loop.
- **Evidence that freezes it:** a second reviewed episode reuses the same mode
  contract, mobile navigation stays simple, accessibility holds, and the shared
  schema prevents content/claim duplication.
- **Evidence to pivot away:** most players must enter the diagnostic layer to
  understand outcomes, mode discovery is poor, hidden truth leaks into play, or
  campaign and laboratory need incompatible information and interaction models.

No option is selected yet. Layer-compatible implementation is the current
reversible default because it preserves option value, not because progressive
disclosure has been validated.

## Experiments and decision gates

1. **Loop experiment:** use one authority-mediated order, a named US or PRC
   implementation chain, visible receipts, one delayed signal/response and one
   same-world alternative. Keep expert-grounded prose visibly draft.
2. **Learning experiment:** test unprompted causal explanation and transfer to
   novel claims, including false inferences caused by the prototype.
3. **Packaging experiment:** expose the retrospective after the core episode.
   Observe whether it is unnecessary, helpful, or indispensable and whether
   players understand its omniscient diagnostic status.
4. **Continuity experiment:** add one causally linked episode only after the
   first three tests. Measure voluntary continuation, recall across the delay
   and whether prior state creates meaningful—not merely disabled—choices.
5. **Audience split experiment:** only if the same shell fails, test the paired
   case as a standalone entry point against the campaign entry point with the
   same mechanism and claim set.

“Favor” means allocate the next bounded experiment. “Freeze” means
provisionally lock that product shape and reject incompatible expansion;
“pivot” means preserve the evidence and test another shape from the current
baseline. Until freeze evidence exists, hold shape-specific architecture. A
shape should become a durable commitment only after repeated evidence, content
operations and mobile/accessibility constraints agree; one positive session is
not enough.

## Reversible architecture defaults

- Use a shared causal kernel, typed event/receipt schema and versioned trace
  record; keep public surfaces as projections rather than alternate truths.
- Keep campaign, dossier and retrospective routing separable even if the first
  build presents them in one sequence.
- Author action labels and one-sentence causal hints, not generic yes/no choices
  or precise-looking resource deltas.
- Keep buttons primary and swipe optional. Allow review/outcome surfaces to
  expand responsively rather than forcing every view into a fixed phone frame.
- Preserve seeded same-world replay, resumability once runs need it, and a
  non-public playtest trace export.
- Let cards present observations and choices; do not make cards the simulation
  clock or give their text arbitrary hidden-state reducers.
- Do not restore aggregate treaty-health, politics, safety-progress or
  survival meters. Do not make art, audio, bespoke gestures or social sharing a
  dependency of the loop proof.

These defaults preserve useful patterns from the retired product at
`0c5262c34c423cc62b68124d30d002b4886b879f`—advisor role, embodied onboarding,
callbacks, deterministic history and multiple input paths—without reviving its
architecture.

## Preservation and pivot protocol

Abandoned experiments are evidence and pattern quarries, not approved design,
source truth or architecture to restore.

Before deleting an unmerged experiment branch:

1. make a clean, self-contained endpoint commit with the hypothesis, evidence
   gate, observed result, validation and claim status;
2. create and push an immutable annotated tag such as
   `experiment/2026-07-11/first-watch-v1`;
3. record the tag **and full commit SHA** in the index below, then verify the
   remote tag resolves;
4. start any successor from the current approved baseline and manually port or
   narrowly cherry-pick validated patterns/tests—do not merge the abandoned
   branch wholesale; and
5. remove the worktree and scratch branch only after the tag and tracked index
   are safely remote. Merging the index to `main` still requires Jörn's review.

Tags provide durable reachability; full SHAs provide immutable identity.
Branches are mutable working pointers and reflogs are not archives. Never move
an archival tag: mint a new version. Revival means a new experiment from the
current baseline with a link to the frozen predecessor.

## Decision cruxes

1. Can concrete receipts and callbacks make the full operational chain legible
   on a simple phone surface?
2. Does same-world comparison improve transfer without breaking role-play or
   confusing diagnostic truth with the Director's knowledge?
3. Do players want an institution in operation after they understand one case,
   or is the high-value product a compact reasoning laboratory?
4. Can campaign and scrutiny audiences share progressive depth, or do they have
   distinct use contexts that justify separate public products?
5. Can one claim/event pipeline support every surface without duplicating or
   silently promoting draft expert content?
