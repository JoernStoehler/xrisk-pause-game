# Compact loop experiment: JOINT-KEY-01

Status: integrated experiment brief, 12 July 2026. This is a project proposal and diagnostic fixture, not an approved product direction, calibrated scenario, or approved player-facing expert content. Jörn must approve the simplified control mechanism and authority gate before participant exposure.

Derived from the [priority ledger](PRIORITY_DECISION_LEDGER.md), [game-design portfolio](../game-design/README.md), [control-crux grammars](../game-design/control-crux-event-grammars.md), [public-discourse priorities](../review/PUBLIC_DISCOURSE_PRIORITIES.md), [success model](SUCCESS_MODEL.md), and [review routing](../review/REVIEW_ROUTING.md). ## Decision

Determine which, if any, compact interaction grammar makes one operational-control distinction both consequential to play and transferable to an unfamiliar case:

> An acknowledged legal halt can deter ordinary conduct while leaving a factual startup path. A separately implemented control can close that path without establishing universal security.

The experiment compares interaction forms, not policy effectiveness in the real world. A favorable result selects a next prototype experiment; it does not freeze product shape or establish fun, learning, demand, or treaty adequacy beyond the tested conditions.

## Diagnostic case

`JOINT-KEY-01` holds constant:

- a declared frontier site and acknowledged legal halt;
- an operator-held unilateral startup credential;
- ordinary personnel deterred under either package;
- one authored residual insider attempt;
- imperfect telemetry and reporting;
- a domestic implementation gate and named implementation latency; and
- unspecified residual routes outside the tested startup path.

The participant chooses between two packages:

1. **Order plus remote response:** the legal halt remains active, but the unilateral startup path remains and response races the attempted start.
2. **Order plus split start authority:** host and ISIA credentials are separately implemented; in this diagnostic world the same attempted start fails closed for lack of the second credential.

The case must never imply that law is useless, split credentials are universally sufficient, or the authored residual attempt is a forecast. Credential theft, collusion, power/network/weight access, restart paths, covert resources, and domestic implementation failure remain outside or unresolved.

## Matched variants

### A — Cabinet decision

1. One-sentence role, objective, site state, and deadline.
2. Up to three optional advisor/evidence views with partial provenance.
3. Persistent focal action: issue one of the two matched packages.
4. Named domestic/ISIA receipt: `accepted`, `narrowed`, `delayed`, `implemented`, or `blocked`.
5. Visible control-state change and later insider callback.
6. Separate retrospective showing the first same-world divergence.

This variant tests whether people, partial views, and executive commitment create felt agency without making an advisor a truth key.

### B — Control-path operation

1. The same role, objective, site state, and deadline.
2. A compact four-row path: legal halt, start authority, interruption/response, observation.
3. Assemble or select one of the same two packages, with a tap alternative to any drag gesture.
4. Run one bounded cold-start control test.
5. The same named receipt, visible state change, later callback, and retrospective.

This variant tests whether manipulating a concrete control path improves agency and law-versus-control explanation without implying a complete security diagram.

### C — Static paired-trace comparator

Present the same package fork, receipt, callback, and first-divergence retrospective as a compact explorable or prose trace. This is a cheaper learning comparator, not a third game candidate. It helps determine whether interaction adds enough agency or transfer to justify its cost.

## Fair-comparison contract

Hold constant across variants:

- fixture, actions, legal authority, domestic gate, receipts, delayed attempt, retrospective, and unresolved routes;
- semantic content and prose budget, including what is optional;
- phone viewport, readable type scale, palette, asset quality, input alternatives, pacing, and instrumentation;
- time and event numbers, visibly labeled as diagnostic fixtures; and
- separation of requested action, implementation, observation, report, Director belief, and retrospective truth.

Measure or equalize taps and exposure time. Do not let advisor charisma, portraits, diagram polish, animation, drag friction, or a richer debrief become the treatment. The retrospective is offered at the same point and must not teach the target answer before the transfer probe.

## Structural preflight

Reject or revise an artifact before human testing if:

- legal order directly sets factual workload or universal compliance;
- Director request is presented as domestic implementation success;
- ordinary deterrable conduct and the residual path collapse into one actor model;
- split credentials appear to secure every access path;
- telemetry, report, analysis, belief, and truth collapse;
- a started run is automatically terminal;
- paired traces resample unrelated state rather than preserving one diagnostic world;
- the retrospective looks like information available to the Director;
- unlabeled probabilities, forecast frequencies, survival/competence meters, or generic upgrades appear;
- one focal action and its receipt cannot fit comfortable progressive phone reading;
- swipe or drag lacks a visible tap alternative; or
- matched variants carry materially unequal claims, prose, visual credibility, or feedback.

Agent and automated preflight can reject these failures. It cannot validate comfortable reading, visual appeal, felt agency, cold comprehension, transfer, or preference.

## Formative human protocol

### Recruitment and assignment

Recruit premise-qualified but treaty-naive participants while preserving strata rather than pooling them: pause/AI-safety community newcomers, technically or politically serious skeptics/low-prior participants, and technical/governance educators or practitioners. Treat any small cohort as formative logistics, not a powered prevalence or effect estimate.

Randomize first exposure within recruitment stratum between A and B. Do not show both before cold-use and transfer measures. If a participant later sees the other variant, counterbalance that exploratory follow-up and record carryover. Test C separately as the cheaper comparator. Keep voluntary and facilitated use contexts separate.

### Silent first use

Use participants' own phones or representative real devices at ordinary settings. Give only the public one-sentence premise; no think-aloud, facilitator rescue, or designer explanation. Record version, device, input, accommodations, stalls, skipped or zoomed text, help-seeking, errors, action latency, quit point, and end reason.

At the natural checkpoint, ask in this fixed order without correctness feedback:

1. “Who were you, and what were you trying to accomplish?”
2. “Tell me what your choice changed, and what happened next.”
3. “Did the choice feel like it mattered? What made it matter or fail to matter?”
4. “What would you want to do next, if anything?”
5. After the retrospective: “What was known, what was only believed or reported, and what remained uncontrolled?”

Capture participant wording before structured coding. Desire to continue is evidence about the sampled moment, not retention or demand.

### Novel transfer probe

Use an unfamiliar actor and control surface while preserving the operational distinction. Ask:

> What action would you take first, what implementation receipt would you seek, and what would that receipt not establish?

Require a free explanation and confidence/uncertainty. Include a tempting law-equals-control interpretation and a proportionate objection that the direct control still does not prove universal security. Code whether the explanation separates authority, implementation receipt, evidence/belief, and factual control; vocabulary recall or selecting the authored package is insufficient. A delayed repeat would strengthen evidence but is not part of the smallest formative pass.

### Qualitative rejection signals

Treat these as lane-specific reasons to reject or revise, not numerical pass thresholds:

- role, objective, or available action cannot be identified without rescue;
- strain, skipping, zooming, or density blocks the first decision;
- choices are described as cosmetic, arbitrary, an opinion quiz, paperwork, or punishment;
- no unprompted account links the action to its immediate consequence;
- the novel case produces confident law-equals-control, two-keys-equals-security, or “choose the author-approved institution” reasoning;
- a material keyboard, screen-reader, low-vision, limited-dexterity, touch, or motion barrier changes essential access; or
- participants have no voluntary next step and say the experience is not worth continuing.

Compare patterns with transcript-backed examples by variant, device, cohort, and use context. Do not invent pass rates from a small formative sample.

## Approval and escalation

Before participant exposure, ask Jörn:

> Is joint credential custody the right simple example of direct factual control for this opening experiment, or should it use power cutoff, model-weight custody, component removal, or another mechanism? Must domestic acceptance gate the Director's order?

His answer changes the action package, authority path, receipt, and possibly fairness of the reveal. It does not select variant A or B.

Reverse or pause the experiment if the approved mechanism cannot fit a meaningful phone-scale decision and receipt, if the diagram necessarily teaches completeness, if both variants fail agency and causal explanation, if C matches their transfer at substantially lower cost, or if a committed facilitator supplies a more informative immediate use context.

## Evidence limits

This experiment cannot estimate misconception prevalence, effect size, retention, demand, virality, institutional reuse, policy influence, treaty feasibility, or survival impact. Immediate explanation is not delayed transfer; agency self-report is not learning; a successful facilitated session is not evidence for an unguided mobile game; and one diagnostic world does not establish real-world frequency or package sufficiency.
